## Deck Structure & Interactive Simulations

1. **01 · INTRO**
   - Agenda (7 modules) & Comprehensive Ion Key Placard.
2. **02 · HARD WATER & SCALE FORMATION**
   - Live thermodynamic boiler tube simulation displaying scale precipitation, thermal insulating boundary layers, and tube rupture failure.
3. **03 · ZEOLITE (PERMUTIT) PROCESS**
   - Cation exchange softening cycle ($Ca^{2+}, Mg^{2+} \to Na^+$) and 10% brine ($NaCl$) regeneration flush.
4. **04 · ION EXCHANGE (DEMINERALISATION)**
   - Dual-bed ion exchange process (Cation bed $RH_2$ followed by Anion bed $R'(OH)_2$) yielding pure demineralised water.
5. **05 · ELECTRODIALYSIS (ED)**
   - 5-compartment electrodialysis stack driven by DC electric field with alternating Cation (CM) and Anion (AM) selective membranes.
6. **06 · REVERSE OSMOSIS (RO)**
   - U-tube membrane separation simulation showing applied hydraulic pressure ($P > \pi = 15–40\text{ bar}$), solute ion rejection, and continuous pure permeate recovery.
7. **07 · PRIMING & FOAMING**
   - Operational boiler trouble simulations demonstrating mechanical priming (water carry-over) and chemical foaming (soap/oil froth), followed by blowdown and antifoaming agent corrections.
8. **08 · PROCESS COMPARISON**
   - 4-technology comparative benchmark matrix (Zeolite vs. Ion Exchange vs. Electrodialysis vs. Reverse Osmosis).

## Features
- **Editorial Technical Design**: High-contrast, clean white paper design aesthetic.
- **Single-Screen Zero-Scroll Layout**: Fits desktop screens (`100dvh`) without vertical scrolling.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Adaptive single-column layout with 2×2 stage button grid and horizontal touch swipe gestures.
- **Speaker Notes**: Dedicated toggleable notes drawer with comprehensive lecture context.
- **Self-Contained**: 100% offline-ready with embedded base64 fonts (`Archivo`, `IBM Plex Sans`, `IBM Plex Mono`).

## Keyboard Shortcuts
- `→` / `Space` / `PageDown`: Next slide

- `1` – `8`: Jump directly to slide
- `Space`: Play / Pause current canvas simulation
- `R`: Reset simulation
- `,` / `.`: Previous / Next simulation stage
- `N`: Toggle speaker notes
- `F`: Fullscreen
